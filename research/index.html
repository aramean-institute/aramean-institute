The Aramean Institute’s research brings together historical, cultural, geographic, linguistic, and archaeological study of Aramean communities and their heritage. Each section presents focused research while preserving the distinctions between historical development, ethnology, material evidence, and sources.

Research Areas

History

Periods, rulers, migrations, conflicts, and political change.

Ethnology

Language, identity, community life, religion, customs, and culture.

Geography

Regions, cities, settlements, landscapes, and diaspora locations.

Archaeology

Sites, inscriptions, artifacts, architecture, and fieldwork.

More research: Language, Literature, Religion, Historical Sources, Figures, Publications, and Statistics.
