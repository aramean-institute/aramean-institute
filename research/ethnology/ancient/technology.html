Tools and Techniques

Ancient Aramean technology is discussed here as tools, materials, and techniques used in ancient production, construction, transport, writing, and food processing. Later technological periods are outside this page.

Agricultural Technologies

Plowing and Cultivation

  • Wooden plows: Early simple designs
  • Metal-tipped plows: Iron implements improving efficiency
  • Hoes and mattocks: Ground preparation tools
  • Sickles and scythes: Grain harvesting implements

Water Management

  • Wells and cisterns: Water storage
  • Water wheels: Mechanical water lifting
  • Aqueducts: Long-distance water transport
  • Dams and reservoirs: Water control systems
  • Irrigation channels: Field water distribution

Storage Technology

  • Granaries for grain preservation
  • Cellars for cool storage
  • Pottery vessels for various goods
  • Oil and wine containers

Textile Technologies

Looms and Spinning

  • Horizontal looms: Ground-level weaving
  • Vertical looms: Wall-mounted frames
  • Spindles and spinning wheels: Yarn production
  • Spindle whorls: Weighted spinning tools

Dyeing Technology

  • Dye extraction from plants and minerals
  • Dye pots and heating systems
  • Mordants for color fixing
  • Fastness and color preservation techniques

Metal Technologies

Ore Processing

  • Ore mining and extraction
  • Smelting furnaces for ore reduction
  • Metal purification techniques
  • Alloying methods for bronze and other metals

Metalworking Tools

  • Anvils and hammers
  • Chisels and cutting tools
  • Molds and casting equipment
  • Forges and heating apparatus

Construction Technologies

Building Methods

  • Stone quarrying and transport
  • Brick and mudbrick production
  • Mortar preparation and application
  • Scaffolding and support systems

Architectural Engineering

  • Arches and vaults: Load-bearing structures
  • Domes: Curved roofing systems
  • Corbeling: Projecting stone support
  • Foundations: Structural stability techniques

Transportation and Communication

Land Transport

  • Wheeled carts: Cargo transport
  • Pack animals: Camel, horse, donkey caravans
  • Roads and routes: Travel infrastructure
  • Way stations: Caravanserais for travelers

Water Transport

  • River boats and barges
  • Sailing vessels
  • Oars and rowing technology
  • Maritime navigation methods

Military Technology

  • Weapons: Swords, spears, bows, arrows
  • Armor: Bronze and later iron protection
  • Fortifications: Walls and defensive structures
  • Siege equipment: Battering rams and siege towers
  • Chariots: Military transport and warfare

Pottery and Ceramic Technology

Pottery Production

  • Potter's wheel: Vessel throwing technology
  • Kilns: Firing technology for hardening
  • Clay preparation: Wedging and processing
  • Slip and glazes: Surface treatments

Writing and Record-Keeping Technologies

Writing Materials

  • Clay tablets and styluses
  • Papyrus and papyrus production
  • Parchment and leather for writing
  • Ink and ink-making techniques

Documentary Systems

  • Administrative record-keeping
  • Legal documentation
  • Religious and literary texts
  • Accounting and economic records

Food Production Technologies

Food Processing

  • Threshing and winnowing: Grain separation
  • Grinding: Flour milling
  • Pressing: Oil and wine production
  • Fermentation: Food preservation and production

Food Storage and Preservation

  • Drying racks for fruit and meat
  • Salt for preservation
  • Smoking and curing
  • Oil and honey preservation

Energy and Power Systems

  • Manpower: Human labor
  • Animal power: Oxen, horses, donkeys
  • Water power: Mills and lifting systems
  • Wind power: Windmills (later period)

Modern household forms and diaspora adaptations are outside this ancient-period page. See Modern Family Structure for current household organization.
