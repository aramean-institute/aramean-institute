Where Arameans Live and Lived

The ancient pages use modern geographic names only as reference points; they do not claim continuous modern identity or settlement.

Ancient Aramean Homeland (Iron Age - Classical Antiquity)

Upper Mesopotamia

The primary region of early Aramean settlement, encompassing:

  • The Syrian steppe and Upper Mesopotamian plains
  • Regions between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers
  • Contemporary Syria, parts of Turkey, and Iraq

The Levantine Corridor

Aramean expansion into Syria and the broader Levant:

  • Damascus and the Anti-Lebanon Mountains
  • The Syrian coast and Palestinian territories
  • Modern-day Lebanon and parts of Israel/Palestine

Mesopotamian Lowlands

Aramean settlement in Babylonian and Assyrian heartland regions, particularly following imperial conquest periods, when Aramean populations were relocated by Assyrian and Persian administrations.
