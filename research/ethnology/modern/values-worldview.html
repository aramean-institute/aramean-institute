Philosophy and Ethics

Aramean values and worldview reflect religious teachings, cultural traditions, and philosophical reflection. Core values guide ethical behavior, relationships, and understanding of reality.

Core Values

Religious and Spiritual Values

  • Faith: Trust in God and divine providence
  • Hope: Expectation of salvation and resurrection
  • Charity (Love): Compassion toward all people
  • Holiness: Pursuit of spiritual purity
  • Obedience: Following divine and moral law

Social and Ethical Values

  • Family loyalty: Commitment to kinship bonds
  • Respect for elders: Honoring age and wisdom
  • Generosity: Sharing with the poor and needy
  • Honesty: Truthfulness in dealings
  • Justice: Fair treatment and equity
  • Hospitality: Welcome to strangers and guests

Virtue Ethics

  • Humility: Modest self-assessment
  • Temperance: Moderation in consumption
  • Courage: Bravery in face of adversity
  • Prudence: Wise judgment
  • Fortitude: Perseverance and endurance

Understanding of God and Cosmos

God Concept

  • Monotheism: One God worship
  • Trinity: Three persons in one God
  • Divine attributes: Omnipotence, omniscience, eternality
  • Divine providence: God's governance of creation

Creation and Cosmos

  • God's creative act ex nihilo (from nothing)
  • Material world as God's creation
  • Humans as image of God
  • Spiritual and material realms interconnection

Human Nature and Destiny

Theological Anthropology

  • Human dignity: Worth through divine image
  • Free will: Human choice and responsibility
  • Sin and fallenness: Moral corruption
  • Redemption possibility: Salvation through Christ

Eschatology (End Times)

  • Final judgment of all people
  • Resurrection of the dead
  • Heaven and hell as eternal states
  • New creation and restoration

Philosophical Worldview

Epistemology (Knowledge)

  • Divine revelation as source of truth
  • Scripture as authoritative knowledge
  • Reason and faith complementarity
  • Experience and tradition as knowledge sources

Metaphysics (Reality)

  • Dualism of spiritual and material
  • Hierarchy of being (God, angels, humans, creation)
  • Time and eternity relationship
  • Change and permanence

Ethics and Morality

Moral Framework

  • Divine command ethics (God's will)
  • Natural law ethics (innate moral sense)
  • Virtue ethics (character development)
  • Consequentialist considerations (outcomes)

Specific Ethical Teachings

  • Sexual ethics and family morality
  • Economic ethics and fair dealing
  • Political ethics and authority
  • Environmental stewardship

Time and History

Historical Consciousness

  • Linear time progression
  • Divine plan in history
  • Redemptive history narratives
  • Hope for future transformation

Cultural Memory

  • Commemoration of past events
  • Ancestral connection and continuity
  • Historical consciousness and identity

Contemporary Value Debates

  • Tradition vs. modernity tensions
  • Individual vs. communal emphasis
  • Adaptation to secular contexts
  • Gender roles and equality
