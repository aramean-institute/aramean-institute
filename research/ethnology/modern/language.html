Modern Aramean language life centers on the continued use of Modern Aramaic dialects in communities spread across the Middle East and the diaspora. This page focuses on the modern phase of Aramaic and its present-day status.

Modern Aramaic is spoken in a diverse family of dialects, including Northeastern, Central, Western, and Jewish varieties. These dialects remain an active part of Aramean identity in both homeland and diaspora communities.
