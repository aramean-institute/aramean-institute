Medieval Aramean language life was shaped by Late Aramaic and Syriac. This page highlights the linguistic world of medieval Arameans, including the use of classical liturgical registers and the regional dialects of Christian communities.

While Arabic became dominant in administration and literature, Aramaic remained central to church worship, local education, and communal identity.
