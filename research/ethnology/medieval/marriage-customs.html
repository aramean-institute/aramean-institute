Medieval Aramean Marriage Traditions

Marriage customs in medieval Aramean Christian society combined ancient wedding practices with new ecclesiastical sacramental frameworks, reflecting the central role of the church in blessing and regulating marriages.

Marriage as Sacrament

The medieval period increasingly emphasized marriage as a Christian sacrament administered by the church, incorporating liturgical elements and ecclesiastical blessing into traditional Aramean wedding practices.

Wedding Ceremonies

Medieval Aramean weddings typically involved both church blessing and community celebration, blending religious and social elements in ceremonies that established new families and kinship networks.
