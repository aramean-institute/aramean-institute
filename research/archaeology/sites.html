This is the main site catalogue for the Archaeology section. It combines documented fieldwork records with broader archaeological locations and site contexts. A site record may include excavation history, research findings, images, or contextual information without implying that every period or area has been excavated equally.

Associations with a polity, language, or community are recorded as historical context. They do not by themselves establish a uniform identity for every period or population represented at a site.
