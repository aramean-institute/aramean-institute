This catalogue brings together documented archaeological fieldwork and material evidence connected with Aramean history. Fieldwork records describe excavation contexts and research priorities; evidence records describe artifacts, architecture, inscriptions, documents, and other material remains. These categories are related but should not be treated as interchangeable.
